01 · THE PROBLEM

The feed grew faster than anyone can read it, and almost none of it matters.

35,364

CVEs published in the first half of 2026. One every 7.4 minutes, up 49.5% year over year. NIST moved the National Vulnerability Database to triage in April.

0.24%

Reached CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities list. Eighty-five of them. The other 35,279 took someone's afternoon anyway.

5 days

Median time to exploit, down from 32. Median time to fix went the other way: 171 days to 252. The gap is where the work is.

95% of vulnerable dependencies are transitive. Under 9.5% are reachable. Triage is mostly the work of proving that something does not matter.

03 · MOVEIT, 2023

British Airways and the BBC never installed MOVEit.

Their payroll provider, Zellis, did. A flaw in a file transfer tool exposed employee bank details at companies that had never heard of the vendor, several contractual layers away from the code.

They found out because Zellis was contractually obligated to tell them. Zellis's clock started the moment Zellis knew, not when the patch shipped, not when the investigation closed. Around 2,700 organisations were in scope before it was over.

If you're B2B SaaS, you are Zellis.
